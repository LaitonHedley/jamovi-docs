---

How to perform an independent samples t-test in jamovi:

1.  You need at least one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous dependent variable, and a <img src="/images/variable-nominal.svg" alt="nominal" style="width: 16px" /> grouping variable with two levels (e.g. treatment / control).

    A correct setup should look similar to this:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_ttest_independent.jpg" alt="data_format_ttest_independent" style="width: 40.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `T-Tests` → `Independent Samples T-Test`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_ttest_independent.jpg" alt="select_ttest_independent" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your outcome variable to `Dependent Variables` and your grouping variable to `Grouping Variable`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_ttest_independent.jpg" alt="add_var_ttest_independent" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  The result is shown in the right panel.

    <img src="/images/jg_output_ttest_independent.jpg" alt="output_ttest_independent" style="width: 70.0%" />

:::tip
Those who have previous experience with SPSS may want to have a look at the [side-by-side-comparison](/spss2jamovi/s2j_ttestis) of how a t-test for independent samples is conducted in SPSS and jamovi.
:::
