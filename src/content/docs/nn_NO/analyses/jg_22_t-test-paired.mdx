title: "t-test: Paired samples"
---

How to perform a paired samples t-test in jamovi:

1.  You need two <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous variables, with each row representing a subject. i.e. the pair of values taken from each row represent paired measurements.

    A correct setup should look similar to this:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_ttest_paired.jpg" alt="data_format_ttest_paired" style="width: 40.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `T-Tests` → `Paired Samples T-Test`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_ttest_paired.jpg" alt="select_ttest_paired" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your two variables to `Paired Variables` (you can drag these one at a time, or select both variables in a pair by holding down the Ctrl-key while clicking on each, before dragging them across).

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_ttest_paired.jpg" alt="add_var_ttest_paired" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  The result is shown in the right panel.

    <img src="/images/jg_output_ttest_paired.jpg" alt="output_ttest_paired" style="width: 70.0%" />

:::tip
Those who have previous experience with SPSS may want to have a look at the [side-by-side-comparison](/spss2jamovi/s2j_ttestps2) of how a t-test for paired samples is conducted in SPSS and jamovi.
:::
