How to perform a factorial ANOVA in jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous dependent variable, and one or more <img src="/images/variable-nominal.svg" alt="nominal" style="width: 16px" /> grouping variables.

    A correct setup should look similar to this:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_anova_factorial.jpg" alt="data_format_anova_factorial" style="width: 50.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `ANOVA` → `ANOVA`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_anova_factorial.jpg" alt="select_anova_factorial" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your outcome variable to `Dependent Variable` and your grouping variables to `Fixed Factors`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_factorial.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_factorial" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  The results are shown in the right panel:

    <img src="/images/jg_output_anova_factorial.jpg" alt="output_anova_factorial" style="width: 70.0%" />
