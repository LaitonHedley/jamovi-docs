How to perform a correlation analysis in jamovi:

1.  For Pearson correlations, you typically need at least two <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px; vertical-align: middle" /> continuous variables (if performing a Spearman or Kendall correlations the variables can be <img src="/images/variable-ordinal.svg" alt="ordinal" style="width: 16px; vertical-align: middle" /> ordinal).

	A correct setup should look similar to this:

	<img src="/images/jg_data_format_correlations.png" alt="data_format_correlations" style="width: 40.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` -> `Regression` -> `Correlation Matrix`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_correlations.png" alt="select_correlations" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop all variables of interest into the box on the right hand side.

	<img src="/images/jg_add_var_correlations.png" alt="add_var_correlations" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  In the options panel, choose the correlation coefficient(s) you want to report (for example Pearson, Spearman, or Kendall). For two continuous variables, keep Pearson selected (the default).

	<img src="/images/jg_options_correlations.png" alt="options_correlations" style="width: 70.0%" />

5.  The result is shown in the right panel.

	<img src="/images/jg_output_correlations.png" alt="output_correlations" style="width: 70.0%" />

:::tip
If you are familiar with SPSS, compare the workflow here: [From SPSS to jamovi: Correlation](/spss2jamovi/s2j_correlation).
:::
