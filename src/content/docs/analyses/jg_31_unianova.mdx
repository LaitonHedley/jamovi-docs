title: "ANOVA: One-Way"
---

How to perform a One-way ANOVA in jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.*" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous dependent variable, and one <img src="/images/variable-nominal.*" alt="nominal" style="width: 16px" /> grouping variable with two or more levels.

    A correct setup should look similar to this:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_unianova.jpg" alt="data_format_unianova" style="width: 40.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `ANOVA` → `One-way ANOVA`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_unianova.jpg" alt="select_unianova" style="width: 45.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your outcome variable to `Dependent Variable` and your grouping variable to `Fixed Factors`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_unianova.jpg" alt="add_var_unianova" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  Select whether your variances are equal or unequal. To test for equality of variancess using Levene's test, tick the box `Equality of variances`.

    <img src="/images/jg_options_unianova.jpg" alt="options_unianova" style="width: 70.0%" />

5.  The results are shown in the right panel:

    <img src="/images/jg_output_unianova.jpg" alt="output_unianova" style="width: 70.0%" />
