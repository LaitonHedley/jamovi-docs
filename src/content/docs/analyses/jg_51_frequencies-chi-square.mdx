---
title: "Chi-square test of association"
---

How to perform a chi-square test of association in jamovi:

1.  You need two <img src="/images/variable-nominal.svg" alt="nominal" style="width: 16px; vertical-align: middle" /> nominal (categorical) variables, with each row representing one case.
    Example: Study method (alone vs group) and exam outcome (pass vs fail), with one row per person.
	A correct setup should look similar to this:
	<img src="/images/jg_data_format_frequencies_chi_square.png" alt="data_format_frequencies_chi_square" style="width: 40.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` -> `Frequencies` -> `Contingency Tables` -> `Independent Samples (Chi-sqaure test of association)`.

	<img src="/images/jg_select_frequencies_chi_square.png" alt="select_frequencies_chi_square" style="width: 70.0%" />

3.  Put one variable in `Rows` and the other in `Columns`.

	<img src="/images/jg_add_var_frequencies_chi_square.png" alt="add_var_frequencies_chi_square" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  The result is shown in the right panel.

	<img src="/images/jg_output_frequencies_chi_square.png" alt="output_frequencies_chi_square" style="width: 50.0%" />
