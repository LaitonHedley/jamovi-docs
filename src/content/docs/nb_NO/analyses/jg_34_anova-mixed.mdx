---
title: "ANOVA med blandingsdesign (dvs. med innersubjekt-faktorer / gjenntatte målinger og mellomsubjekt-faktorer)"
---

Slik gjennomfører du en ANOVA med blandingsdesign i jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous variable for each within-subject measurement (e.g. pre / post) and at least one <img src="/images/variable-nominal.svg" alt="nominal" style="width: 16px" /> grouping variable (between-subjects-factor) with at least two levels (e.g., treatment / control, gender).

    Et riktig oppsett skal se slik ut:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_anova_mixed.jpg" alt="data_format_anova_mixed" style="width: 50.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `ANOVA` → `Repeated Measures ANOVA`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_anova_repeated.jpg" alt="select_anova_repeated" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  In the box `Repeated Measures Factors`: write the name of your outcome variable (e.g., `My_scale`) and name the levels for each measurement occasion (e.g., `Pre`, `Post` and `12 month follow-up`). The images below illustrates this.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_repeated_naming_1.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_repeated_naming_1" style="width: 30.0%" /> `→` <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_repeated_naming_2.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_repeated_naming_2" style="width: 30.0%" />

4.  Drag and drop your outcome variables to their respective cells in `Repeated Measures Cells`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_repeated.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_repeated" style="width: 70.0%" />

5.  Move your grouping variable(s) to `Between Subject Factors`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_mixed.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_mixed" style="width: 35.0%" />

6.  The result is shown in the right panel:

    <img src="/images/jg_output_anova_mixed.jpg" alt="output_anova_mixed" style="width: 70.0%" />
