---
title: "t-test for ett utvalg"
---

Slik gjennomfører du en t-test for ett utvalg i jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous variable and a mean to test against (typically the mean being tested is zero).

    Et riktig oppsett skal se slik ut:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_ttest_onesample.jpg" alt="data_format_ttest_onesample" style="width: 30.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `T-Tests` → `One Sample T-Test`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_ttest_onesample.jpg" alt="select_ttest_onesample" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your dependent variable to `Dependent Variables`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_ttest_onesample.jpg" alt="add_var_ttest_onesample" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  Locate the section `Hypothesis` further down, this is where you enter the mean you want to test against.

    <img src="/images/jg_options_ttest_onesample.jpg" alt="options_ttest_onesample" style="width: 70.0%" />

5.  The result is shown in the right panel.

    <img src="/images/jg_output_ttest_onesample.jpg" alt="output_ttest_onesample" style="width: 70.0%" />
