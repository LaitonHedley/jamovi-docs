---
title: "ANOVA for gjentatte målinger"
---

Slik gjennomfører du en ANOVA for gjentatte målinger i jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> continuous dependent variable for each measurement occasion.

    Et riktig oppsett skal se slik ut:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_anova_repeated.jpg" alt="data_format_anova_repeated" style="width: 40.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `ANOVA` → `Repeated Measures ANOVA`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_anova_repeated.jpg" alt="select_anova_repeated" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  In the box `Repeated Measures Factors`: write the name of your outcome variable (e.g., `My_scale`) and name the levels for each measurement occasion (e.g., `Pre`, `Post` and `12 month follow-up`). The images below shows the box with default values (left) and when the values has been set (right).

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_repeated_naming_1.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_repeated_naming_1" style="width: 35.0%" /> `→` <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_repeated_naming_2.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_repeated_naming_2" style="width: 35.0%" />

4.  Drag and drop your outcome variables to their respective cells in `Repeated Measures Cells`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_anova_repeated.jpg" alt="add_var_anova_repeated" style="width: 70.0%" />

5.  To test the equality of the variance with the Levene test, open the `Assumption Checks` drop-down menu and set the `Homogeneity test` tick box. It is also a good idea to check that the variances at the different steps of the repetition factor are the same (Sphericity). To do so, open the `Assumption  Checks` drop-down menu and set the `Sphericity tests` tick box.

6.  The results are shown in the right panel:

    <img src="/images/jg_output_anova_repeated.jpg" alt="output_anova_repeated" style="width: 70.0%" />
