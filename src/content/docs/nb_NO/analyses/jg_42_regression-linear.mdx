title: "Lineær regresjon"
---

Slik gjennomfører du en lineær regresjon i jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> dependent variable, and at least one <img src="/images/variable-continuous.svg" alt="continuous" style="width: 16px" /> predictor variable (aka a covariate).

    Et riktig oppsett skal se slik ut:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_regression_linear.jpg" alt="data_format_regression_linear" style="width: 25.0%" />

2.  Select `Analyses` → `Regression` → `Linear Regression`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_regression_linear.jpg" alt="select_regression_linear" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your dependent variable to `Dependent Variable` and your explanatory variable to `Covariates` if it is continuous or to `Factors` if it is categorical.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_regression_linear.jpg" alt="add_var_regression_linear" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  The result is shown in the right panel:

    <img src="/images/jg_output_regression_linear.jpg" alt="output_regression_linear" style="width: 50.0%" />

:::tip
Those who have previous experience with SPSS may want to have a look at the [side-by-side-comparison](/spss2jamovi/s2j_linreg) of how a linear regression is conducted in SPSS and jamovi.
:::
