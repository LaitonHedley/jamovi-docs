---
title: "Logistisk regresjon"
---

Slik gjennomfører du en logistisk regresjon i jamovi:

1.  You need one <img src="/images/variable-nominal.svg" alt="nominal" style="width: 16px" /> <img src="/images/variable-ordinal.svg" alt="ordinal" style="width: 16px" /> categorical dependent variable (nominal or ordinal), and at least one continuous explanatory variable.

    Et riktig oppsett skal se slik ut:

    <img src="/images/jg_data_format_regression_logistic.jpg" alt="data_format_regression_logistic" style="width: 25.0%" />

2.  Logistic regression can be found by selecting `Analyses` → `Regression`. If the outcome variable is nominal (as in the above image), select `2 Outcomes` if it has 2 steps / different values, or `N outcomes` if it has *more* than 2 steps. If the outcome variable is ordinal (e.g., low, medium, high), select `Ordinal Outcomes`.

    <img src="/images/jg_select_regression_logistic.jpg" alt="select_regression_logistic" style="width: 40.0%" />

3.  Drag and drop your dependent variable to `Dependent Variable` and your predictor to `Covariates`.

    <img src="/images/jg_add_var_regression_logistic.jpg" alt="add_var_regression_logistic" style="width: 70.0%" />

4.  Scroll down to the `Model Coefficients` drop-down-menu and check the options `Odds ratio` and `Confidence interval`.

    <img src="/images/jg_options_regression_logistic.jpg" alt="options_regression_logistic" style="width: 70.0%" />

5.  The result is shown in the right panel:

    <img src="/images/jg_output_regression_logistic.jpg" alt="output_regression_logistic" style="width: 50.0%" />

:::tip
Those who have previous experience with SPSS may want to have a look at the [side-by-side-comparison](/spss2jamovi/s2j_logreg) of how a logistic regression is conducted in SPSS and jamovi.
:::

::: admonition
Further help from the community resources

A little more comprehensive introduction into this statistical method is provided by this two videos, explaining logistic regression with [two levels](https://www.youtube.com/embed/s7GL0z-3ymA?list=PLkk92zzyru5OAtc_ItUubaSSq6S_TGfRn) (to predict, e.g., gender or clinical vs. control group) and with [more than two levels](https://www.youtube.com/embed/nuyEUEBf-GQ?list=PLkk92zzyru5OAtc_ItUubaSSq6S_TGfRn) (to predict, e.g., food preferences: fast food, healthy food, high protein food, vegan food, etc.).
:::
